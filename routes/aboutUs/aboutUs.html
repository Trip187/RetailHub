Track Your Order

Every order moves through four stages, and you can check where yours is at any time using the order number sent to your email after checkout.

  1. Order Placed — payment confirmed and your order enters our queue.
  2. Processing — your items are picked, checked, and packed.
  3. Out for Delivery — your order is on its way with a courier.
  4. Delivered — your order has arrived.
